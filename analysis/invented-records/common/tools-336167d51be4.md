# Tools the seat was offered (names and descriptions)

## `position`

Take the floor with your independent analysis and preliminary vote.
Round 1 only — you have not yet heard the other councillors.

## `respond`

Respond to the other councillors during deliberation. Signal
`ready_to_vote` when you have nothing further to add.

## `vote`

Cast your final vote. Only after the Steward has called the vote.

## `ask_clerk`

Ask the Clerk to read specific comments from the proposal's thread
verbatim into the record, by their [C#] numbers. You may then
continue your turn.
