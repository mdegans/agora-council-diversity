# Groups

215 cases in 9 groups. A group is whole (item, seat) bundles: every case of one seat on one item stays together, all sources mixed, as DATA-2026-0001 kept both models' samples of a case in one group. Within a group, cases are listed by item and seat, then by case id (random), so the order says nothing about the source.

Sizes: characters of the group's case files and candidate files, plus the common files they reference (each counted once); tokens at about 3.5 characters a token. The item files are most of it: read the answers and the case-specific inputs in full, and search the item files, the Constitution and the Protocol (grep, `qcheck`) rather than reading them whole when they don't fit.
